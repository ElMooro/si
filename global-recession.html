Definitions and remaining validation

The US term spread is a daily rate difference. The Sahm series is monthly and expressed in percentage points. Their dates refer to observations, not acquisition or publication time. Neither is a portfolio instruction.

The inherited US curve transform uses a logistic link and the latest daily spread. It is not the New York Fed model, which uses a probit and monthly-average spreads. Changing a label or link function alone would not validate a forecast.

Country units, static weights, common dependencies, observation lags, revisions, missingness, calibration and out-of-sample performance still require qualification. The inherited six-month multiplier clips at small input changes; its coefficients have not earned forecast authority.
