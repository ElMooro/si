Evidence and limits

The browser verifies the immutable output, selected issuer history and displayed arithmetic. Complete original-response replay and an independent rational-arithmetic check run on AWS. Raw provider responses remain protected; hashes, source rows, fields and acquisition times identify the exact retained inputs.

Every reported ticker/CIK pair is compared with a dated, retained SEC current ticker index. Conflicting, ambiguous and unmapped identities remain visible with calculations withheld. A current match does not establish historical issuer continuity or confirm individual filing values. Multiple current labels for one CIK are not independent confirmations. Index membership is not established by this captured universe.

Reconciliation residuals measure consistency between provider fields, which may share a formula. Zero residuals do not certify an audit. These measurements have no qualified fraud probability, financial-strength grade, trading signal or position size.
