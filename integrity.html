Why this board exists. The fundamental-census walk ran at 25% completion for months and 90 engines fired twice on every tick. This board runs the checks that would have caught them, weekly, against every function in the fleet — and flags what is NEW since the last accepted baseline, so a known backlog never drowns a fresh failure.
