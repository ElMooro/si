Interpretation and limits

The primary reading is the sample standard deviation of 30 successive numeric-observation changes, expressed in basis points per observation step. The window contains 31 numeric observations. Its actual calendar distance, missing rows and largest interval are shown. A gap above seven days withholds the current statistic.

Annualization assumes 252 comparable observation steps per year. This assumption is separate from an exchange trading calendar. The z-score and midrank percentile compare the current estimate with 252 preceding rolling windows, excluding the current window. Those overlapping windows are dependent; a z-score is not a Gaussian probability or a forecast. A flat baseline has an undefined z-score.

Observation age has a seven-calendar-day ceiling; source acquisitions expire after 26 hours. Release-calendar freshness remains unverified. Values also expire while this page stays open. Provider history is the acquired current vintage, not a point-in-time backtest. Full retained history does not imply full lifetime coverage.

MOVE is a separate option-implied index. Realized rate-change dispersion is not a synthetic replacement or a MOVE-equivalent index. A public quote needs a reviewed instrument identity; even then, parity with the official ICE feed remains unverified.

Portfolio consequence: unavailable. These measurements have no qualified forecast, Calls vote, target weight or execution permission. WAIT means abstain. Position decisions require validated predictive evidence, holdings, costs and portfolio risk constraints.
