Definitions and replay

Reported OI is summed separately for calls and puts. Gamma coefficients sum vendor gamma × reported open interest × the verified 100-share deliverable. Delta coefficients use vendor delta with the same units and population. Missing or invalid fields are excluded only from affected calculations; true zero remains zero. Each cell shows its own coverage.

Long calls and long puts have positive gamma. Call/put classification does not reveal who is long or short. The previous assumed dealer signs, flip levels, support/resistance labels and squeeze rankings are retired. Whole predecessor history is retained as unqualified model output, with its original meaning.
