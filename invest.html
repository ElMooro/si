Invest — Why Leave SPX?

Every industry and stock below had to earn its place. Tier 1 requires a real, multi-leg-corroborated leading trade/commodity signal — one noisy print doesn't count. Tier 2 requires the industry's risk-adjusted expected return over SPX to clear both an information-ratio floor and a minimum excess-return floor — SPX is the default; the industry has to beat it, not just match it — cross-checked against real institutional positioning (sector fund-flow conviction, industry-level insider buying). Tier 3 only ranks stocks inside industries that passed Tier 2, blending fundamentals (backlog, valuation, catalysts) with the same institutional-edge signals hedge funds track: smart-money convergence, credit-leads-equity, short-squeeze setups, hiring velocity, and estimate-revision momentum — and a stock has to clearly beat its own industry ETF to be picked over just buying it.
