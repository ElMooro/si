What the measurements establish

  • Values use official native FRED units. Millions and billions are never inferred from a category. The source definition takes precedence over a requested label.
  • Month, quarter and year changes use calendar baselines. Monthly series have no weekly change; quarterly surveys have no monthly change. Percent levels change in percentage points, separately from relative growth.
  • Charts show the newest 60 retained observations with date spacing and missing-value gaps. Complete fetched responses remain available through the original-evidence links; a bounded source query is not an all-time history.
  • One- and five-year z-scores describe a current retrieved vintage using population standard deviation. History must reach the calendar cutoff. Missing or constant history does not become a zero z-score. No crisis probability or return forecast follows from these statistics.
  • Observation age, source acquisition and desk generation are separate clocks. Exact release calendars and historical as-known-at vintages remain unverified. The page rechecks its age ceilings while open.
  • HQM spot yields and Treasury constant-maturity yields are different constructions. No executable spread or matched credit premium is inferred from their latest values. Repo remains separately labeled unverified context in the full packet.
