📚 Methodology

The Liquidity & Credit Engine pulls 51 series from FRED across five categories, every 6 hours, and computes per-series WoW · MoM · QoQ · YoY · z(1y) · z(5y) alongside legacy threshold labels. Original-source, units and calendar-baseline validation for these calculations is pending; historical crisis examples do not establish calibration.

The composite score blends the worst category state (70% weight) with the average state across all categories (30% weight) and translates into a regime: CALM WATCH ELEVATED ACUTE STRESS CRISIS.

This desk is research-only. Its threshold labels and numerical score have no validated Calls or sizing authority. Portfolio actions remain unavailable until source verification, independent model evaluation and a verified portfolio snapshot support them.
