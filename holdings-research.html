Permanent source snapshot

A change in reported market value is not a purchase or sale. Public holdings do not reveal interim trades, corporate-action adjustments, a manager’s complete portfolio, or current positioning. This research carries no Calls, sizing or execution authority.

Keep report periods separate

Managers below belong to the retained roster. Older reports remain visible but are excluded from the current report-period cohort.

Compare reported securities

SH means reported shares; PRN means reported principal. Puts and calls are kept separate from outright holdings. Missing prior data is unavailable, not zero. “Newly present” and “absent” describe public disclosure, not verified entry or exit trades.

Filing and amendment evidence

A restatement replaces its predecessor. An additional-holdings amendment is reviewed separately; an incomplete or ambiguous chain cannot support comparisons. Acceptance time, filing date and report date are different clocks.

Portfolio interpretation

These disclosures can identify securities to research and reveal dated concentration. They do not establish a return forecast or a position size. To quantify your own exposure, use the explicit portfolio scenario calculator with your entered price, currency, cash-income and cost assumptions.

No account is read or changed. Shared discretion can put the same economic position in multiple managers’ disclosures. Adding their reported values does not establish new capital entering a security.
