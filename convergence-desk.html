Calculation definitions and limitations

Base Compound score = sum of selected system scores × [1 + 0.5 × (system count − 1)], rounded to one decimal. The multiplier is a declared heuristic; it does not measure independent confirmation.

Lifecycle-adjusted score = base score × rounded mean first-seen decay × mean family prior, rounded to one decimal. Family priors are flow 1.35, fundamental 1.15 and technical 1.00; unmapped systems use 1.00. Decay half-lives are 5, 30 and 4 days respectively; unmapped systems use 10 days. These constants are uncalibrated.

First-seen age describes the screen’s lifecycle, not the underlying observation’s freshness. Missing, invalid or future first-seen dates leave the adjusted score unavailable. Each record retains its calculation inputs.

Historical percentiles compare base scores with compatible retained cohorts from the prior 90 calendar days. Today, future dates, duplicate days, invalid populations and incompatible calculation revisions are excluded. The top-400 daily population is selected and is not the market universe.

Reported reversal context is descriptive. The change between two undated sparkline positions cannot establish a five-day return or entry quality. A packet generation timestamp does not certify source freshness.
