Methodology — how a desk reads this board

Families. Systems are normalized and mapped to three orthogonal families — flow (options flow, smart-money/13F, insider, congress, dark-pool), fundamental (rev-accel, eps-velocity, PEAD, deep-value, no-brainers), technical (momentum, vol-squeeze, pre-pump). Two cousins in one family can't fake independent confirmation; PRIME = all three families present with ≥4 systems — the generalized form of the triad that called AAPL/GOOGL/MSFT.

Desk score = compound × freshness × avg family prior (flow 1.35 · fundamental 1.15 · technical 1.00; basis disclosed per row as family_priors_v1 — realized-alpha weights join from signal-backtest as it accrues combo grades). Freshness decays each (symbol,system) from its first-seen date with family half-lives (flow 5d · technical 4d · fundamental 30d).

Chase guard. entry_quality uses the reversal radar's 5-day tape proxy: EXTENDED (≥+8% in ~5d) is flagged because the house event-study measured chasing at 38.7% hit-rate and −148bps — the desk buys FRESH or PULLBACK, watches EXTENDED. Regime stamps risk-gate posture and the market-turn net so every flag is conditioned on its environment.
