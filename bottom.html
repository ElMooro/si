BOTTOM · the Wyckoff bottom desk

A bottom is a process, not a price. The engine watches every asset with real volume in the warehouse — stocks, bond / gold / metal / commodity / currency / country / sector wrappers and spot crypto — for the sequence the paper describes: a selling climax (panic on climactic volume), the automatic rally (the bounce the crowd buys), and the secondary test (price returns to the low; the signal is the silence in the volume). It buys nothing at the level: the trigger is a close above the high of the candle that made the test low, the stop sits just under that low, and every historical sequence is graded from the tape so the base rates below are measured, not quoted.
