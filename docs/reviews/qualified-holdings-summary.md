## Meaning and conservative exclusions

Unique funds count once per exact existing security identity. Current effective-date cohorts and ordered prior/current date pairs never mix. Exact counts apply only to the named eligible cohort, with eligible/configured denominators and retained source references/clocks. Complete returned pagination, valid acquisition/expiry, one nonfuture effective date, nonfuture processing date and zero identity/field-error counts are all required. This is completeness of the reported source response, not independent verification of the entire portfolio.

Raw observed counts remain separate. Fresh known presence can be a lower bound despite partial identity coverage, but does not establish absence, denominator eligibility or a global rank. Stale observations remain raw only. Same-date revisions cannot enter comparisons. Ties use exact identity. Counts include reported zero/short rows: they are reported membership observations, not long ownership, buys/sells or constituent capital flows.

Quantity/weight observations remain in referenced native comparisons with original units; summary does not aggregate them or reinterpret weight scale. Direct source asset/security labels and ticker aliases are copied, explicitly unverified; configured fund tags also remain unverified. No inferred stock/fund class, fund-of-funds expansion, overlapping basket netting, leverage/inverse netting, corporate-action adjustment or aggregate dollars. Consumers must not market this as all-market ownership. Future UI classification/filtering still requires review and must preserve these exclusions.

Summary qualification is pinned to the retained compilation clock. Future browsers must enforce cohort expiry; replay does not refresh the data. Present canonical 294/300 fresh complete is parent-supplied evidence, not a measurement from this environment; it is not automatically 294 identity-qualified funds. Six unavailable funds retain reasons, not zeros. Cutoffs are current and 30 days earlier, not daily changes.
