# Katlin optional research vote qualification

The current regime-composite UNAVAILABLE and credit-stress RESEARCH_ONLY labels were both interpreted as GREEN/25 risk votes. Vol-regime's actual COMPLACENT/NORMAL/CONCERNED/PANIC/UNKNOWN vocabulary also fell through to GREEN/25. An offline counterexample with otherwise valid authority/funding moved from SELECTIVE/cap65 to FULL_RISK/cap100 when either withheld regime or credit packet was added.

These three optional inputs now abstain from Katlin risk voting. Their complete JSON-compatible received research packets, original timestamps and canonical parsed-input hashes remain under `war_room.research_context`; hashes identify parsed evidence, not stored original bytes or validated models. Invalid non-JSON measurements cannot be republished as valid research. Publication freshness is explicitly distinct from observation freshness, with no future-clock tolerance. Credit also uses the existing typed measurement adapter. No missing optional source becomes a new required hold.

This change does not introduce a flags-only qualification escape. `aws/shared/credit_research.py` explicitly has no registered qualified signal and rejects self-asserted eligibility. `justhodl-regime-composite` publishes UNVALIDATED_DESCRIPTIVE_HEURISTIC, WAIT/abstain and false permissions. There is no independently qualified legacy investment contract for either source. Their descriptive measurements remain available; no approved qualified behavior is removed.

Volatility policy remains unresolved: its producer uses research scores 0/25/60/100 and classification thresholds 15/40/70, but those are not an approved Katlin exposure policy. The original Katlin launch commit `4079adbf4` already mismatched the producer vocabulary. No new volatility risk thresholds are invented. Admitting these inputs in the future requires a separately reviewed qualified contract and, for volatility, an explicit Katlin mapping.

Only `war_room` changes in production. Required funding's NONE/WATCH/SHORTAGE SIGNAL mappings and freshness-bound expiry, raw-gate and Khalid authority holds, all other legs, scoring, basket sizing, optional-source criticality, schedules and shared policy remain unchanged. Both daily publication and permission refresh use the same boundary. Other descriptive/withheld sources outside these three are not silently reclassified in this repair.

Regression coverage includes missing/malformed/unknown inputs, forged eligibility, nonfinite and boolean values, measured zero, stale/naive/future clocks, all native volatility states, weighted-risk and basket dilution, original research preservation, funding/raw-gate holds, and actual daily/refresh publication through in-memory IO. Existing native funding matrices and whole-function identity guards remain active.
