# Reported Bitcoin sentiment

Alternative.me describes its current Fear & Greed Index as Bitcoin sentiment on a 0–100 scale. Its source: https://alternative.me/crypto/fear-and-greed-index/ . Provider timestamps are retained as reported observation clocks; independently verified first-publication and point-in-time availability remain unavailable. Attribution appears next to each revised display.

The two existing provider requests are captured separately, with every returned source row, UTC timestamp, source pointer and unresolved duplicate retained. The existing CoinGecko price request remains an explicitly separate original attempt and retains the predecessor prerequisite: it is skipped after an unusable recent sentiment response. Price-derived synthetic prehistory is never provider index history. No older rows are sampled away. Current means latest resolved source time, never the first supplied row; missing, boolean, fractional or invalid values are not 50 or zero. Copies disagreeing on the latest clock/value withhold current. Means require a complete 7/30 UTC-calendar-day window, with an explicit integer numerator, denominator, members and half-even two-decimal rounding. Conflicting duplicate clocks and date gaps cannot silently change the sample.

Whole bounded original response bytes, or explicitly incomplete prefixes, request/acquisition metadata, projections and three reviewed compiler files are conditionally stored by SHA-256 under `data/crypto-sentiment-research/`. Complete readback and exact replay precede publication. Retention is not Object Lock. The 8 MiB response and 50,000-row projection limits refuse oversized populations as a whole; neither proves a runtime SLO. Transport and storage check the remaining execution budget and do not retry requests. Consumer contexts independently recompute the complete supplied original and bind its capture address; they do not fetch the stored archive or assert its current availability.

All Calls, sizing, execution, forecast, source-authenticity and point-in-time permissions remain false. The allocator awards no sentiment points; cycle risk has no sentiment risk mapping; no newly invented contrarian confidence enters the outcome ledger. Historical ledger entries are unchanged. Crypto and Desk use typed descriptive values, source attribution, observation dates and explicit unknowns. The Crypto inspector retains both complete source-row populations; the chart uses actual time spacing and breaks missing-date segments. Browser checks concern presentation consistency, not an independent original replay. Existing schedules, resources, source endpoints and unrelated factors remain unchanged. Native release, served bytes, browser behavior and normally produced original replay need separate acceptance.
