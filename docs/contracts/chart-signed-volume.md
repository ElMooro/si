# Signed volume presentation and the close-location estimate

The quantity formerly labeled only Δ in the main quote is labeled CLV × Vol. Its tooltip states the formula `(2*(close-low)/(high-low)-1)*reported volume`, identifies it as an OHLC estimate and distinguishes it from measured buyer-versus-seller flow. Its sign depends on the close's position within the bar range, independently of the open or preceding close. This is a descriptive transformation, not evidence of institutional flow, aggression, accumulation or an investment signal. Original volume units and source completeness remain unqualified.

The calculation requires finite primitive high, low and close values in an ordered, nonzero range and a finite nonnegative reported volume. Unknown or typed values, reversed ranges and closes outside the range remain unavailable. A flat bar no longer manufactures zero for an undefined close-location ratio. A valid nonflat bar with measured zero volume remains zero. Overflowing ranges, underflowing nonzero calculations and an unrepresentable non-midpoint multiplier are withheld. The existing arithmetic is retained for ordinary complete nonflat frames. Input records are not changed or discarded.

Signed presentation retains the minus sign before a negative magnitude, including K/M/B/T formatting. Unavailable values do not acquire a numeric zero or plus sign; signed zero is presented as zero. The same formatter is used for the existing QR summary display. This repairs presentation only for that summary: it does not certify its acquisition, symbol ownership, side assignment, timestamp, total or upstream missing-value coercions.
