# Retained-chart OBV and volume arithmetic

Stage 504 repairs descriptive on-balance volume on the retained chart frame. Up closes add that bar's reported volume, down closes subtract it, and equal closes contribute no change. This matches the recurrence in [Fidelity's OBV definition](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/technical-analysis/technical-indicator-guide/obv); it is not evidence of predictive performance or measured signed transactions.

The first valid retained close is an explicit zero anchor. The first bar's volume is not signed or accumulated; it may be unavailable without inventing a first-bar contribution. The zero is a chart-history convention, not measured money flow. Subsequent volumes must be primitive finite nonnegative numbers and closes must be primitive finite numbers. Missing, coerced or negative volume, invalid close, nonfinite arithmetic or an absorbed nonzero increment withhold the remaining cumulative chain. No later bar restarts or repairs the accumulator implicitly. Full received time positions stay present as whitespace after the break; unordered or malformed time coordinates prevent plotting.

The pane labels a broken current chain Unavailable, hides the prior segment's last numeric axis value, and states its zero anchor and unverified volume/source basis. A corrected complete frame can calculate again; prior unknown rows are not silently dropped. The separate stock-desk prior/inclusive volume ratios also withhold a nonzero numerator whose floating-point ratio underflows to zero. Legitimate zero and all existing observation-window/trace fields remain intact.

These repairs operate on the retained frame, after the legacy provider decoder. They cannot restore missingness already defaulted upstream, establish volume units, session completeness, corporate-action basis, first availability or original-provider equivalence. No Calls, forecast or sizing permission changes. Exact signed integer fixtures, invalid/zero/overflow inputs, prefix invariance, unchanged-function preservation and offline real-library browser cases are the validation boundary. No actual private/current-consumer data or producer execution is involved. All ten institutional workstreams remain open.
