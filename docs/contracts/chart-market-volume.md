# Market volume availability and core calculation guards

The market decoder accepts explicit finite nonnegative numeric volume. Missing, null, boolean, numeric-string, negative and non-finite values remain unavailable. The volume/v/vol/Volume aliases must agree when multiple fields are supplied; a generic price/value field is never a volume fallback. Numeric strings require a separately qualified provider adapter before acceptance. An absent Yahoo volume array does not discard otherwise usable price rows. Actual provider schemas and original source coverage remain unverified by these invented fixtures.

Price normalization, rounding, daily/grouped aggregation, weekly views and row replacement preserve unavailable volume. A grouped sum is unavailable when an operand is unknown or floating arithmetic overflows or loses a positive contribution. A deliberately volume-withheld crypto history supplement uses null. The source records, price values and existing selection rules are not qualified by this change.

The core volume histogram retains missing dates as whitespace. Its moving average uses exactly twenty observations including the current bar, requires every volume in that window and plots separate valid runs. This differs from RVOL's preceding-twenty denominator. Genuine zero remains zero. An unavailable final value cannot show an earlier numeric axis tag. RVOL and OBV retain their previously qualified window and cumulative-chain rules.

The older VWMA, VWAP, CVD, MFI, A/D, CMF, Force, volume oscillator, EOM and anchored/period/swing VWAP implementations are conservatively withheld for incomplete retained frames. This whole-frame admission guard is not individual rolling-window recovery, causal historical qualification, predictive validation or a complete numerical audit of their legacy formulas. Their complete-frame arithmetic remains unchanged. Other annotation, tape, pattern and overlay modules remain separately unqualified and are not granted authority by these changes.

Quote, detail, data-window and tape labels distinguish unavailable quantities from measured zero, preserving small fractional and signed derived values. Missing VWAP cannot become a zero-percent deviation; missing bar volume cannot become a zero delta estimate. Quote and hover state is cleared on replacement. Main-series ownership is cleared before removeSeries callbacks run, preventing a callback from rebuilding the preceding frame's volume profile under the new symbol.

Tests retain complete invented packet shapes, the decoder predecessor and the intermediate callback failure with its complete diagnostic source. Offline browsers intercept every request and use the pinned real drawing library. This contract does not certify actual provider availability, transformed-candle volume attribution, calendar completeness, units, account positions, investment recommendations or every chart extension. All ten institutional workstreams remain open.
