## Drawing-resource lifecycle found during regression

The broader real-library axis test exposed asynchronous “Object is disposed” failures. Retained complete error stacks and browser state accompany a direct reproduction: a removed drawing object is resized again by retained callbacks. `mkChart` now disconnects its own ResizeObserver, clears both pending fit timers and removes its window resize listener before delegating to the library's remove method. Already queued callbacks are inert after removal, and repeated removal is idempotent. Other charts retain their own callbacks. The chart API, initial dimensions, active resize behavior and original chart options are preserved.

Seven focused lifecycle cases pass; the complete predecessor fails six with the same assertions. A real-library test creates and removes a visible chart before its callbacks settle and records every resize call; the predecessor still resizes removed objects, while the candidate has none. Five full desktop/mobile repetitions pass. This qualifies the custom resources owned by `mkChart`; it does not qualify every asynchronous auxiliary panel or internal third-party-library resource.
