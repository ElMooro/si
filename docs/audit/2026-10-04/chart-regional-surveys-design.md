# Regional Federal Reserve survey histories

This additive review preserves the original 77 Chicago and Kansas City definitions and adds 567 individually qualified fields from nine public files published by the New York, Philadelphia, Richmond and Dallas Federal Reserve Banks. The catalogue has 644 definitions in eleven datasets. Eleven watchlist identifiers receive explicitly qualified official-source alternatives. Neither vendor equivalence nor full upstream historical completeness is asserted.

The original CSV or OOXML response, source URL, acquisition clock, checksum, exact column, original numeric lexeme and original monthly reference cell remain inspectable. Strict source-specific headers and worksheets prevent a publisher layout change from silently moving a series. The legacy Dallas .xls filenames contain OOXML; no BIFF format is guessed. Philadelphia May-68 means May 1968. Dallas's published April-14 and one numeric Excel date among service-sector text dates are explicitly supported. Chart coordinates are month anchors, not publication clocks. Current revised and seasonally revised histories are not point-in-time release vintages.

NY and Philadelphia response shares retain percent-of-respondents units; diffusion indexes retain index-point units. Published diffusion values are used directly, never recomputed from rounded category percentages. Current and six-month expected responses are separate identifiers. Philadelphia capital expenditure is an expectations field. Dallas Bact, production, new orders, revenue and finished-goods inventories remain distinct; the F in Fgi does not mean future. Seasonal and unadjusted histories cannot silently substitute for each other. Adjusted values outside unadjusted bounds remain flagged; missing or invalid source values remain null with their original text.

Richmond price fields use the publisher's annualized-percent-change unit and are not diffusion indexes. Their expected-price horizon remains unverified rather than adopting the generic six-month convention. The October 2025 services-to-non-manufacturing rename and potential composition change are disclosed. sa_mfg_emp_e has contradictory adjustment definitions and nsa_mfg_emp_e is absent from the official dictionary; both remain withheld from the directory and routes. The published manufacturing composite is preserved without recomputing rounded components.

Original official all-series data were independently compared with CSV and openpyxl readers: 243,427 new field observations, including 226,373 numeric values. The NY and Philadelphia diffusion-only publications independently reconcile to corresponding all-series fields. This is offline evidence until separately deployed and replayed through the public chart API.

Dataset-level shared admission and retention reuse nine downloads across 567 fields. A refusal blocks the affected source; no retry, different host, market-price fallback or zero filling follows. Workbooks never execute formulas or fetch embedded relationships. Formula-cache values are explicitly marked. The adapter conveys research observations and has no Calls, sizing, trading-volume or OHLC authority. USLEI, ISM New York, NAR pending-home-sales and the held Richmond fields are not guessed from similar labels.
