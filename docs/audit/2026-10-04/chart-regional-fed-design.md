# Official regional Federal Reserve histories

The reviewed adapter exposes seven Chicago Fed National Activity Index series and 70 Kansas City manufacturing survey series. Complete originals are retained with exact definitions and acquisition receipts. An independent openpyxl replay checks 26,208 source rows, of which 25,598 are numeric. Missing observations remain null. This is source research and candidate evidence, not live deployment proof.

CFNAI, its four category contributions, its three-month moving average and its weighted diffusion index remain distinct. The official historical CSV and separate official XLSX values agree; they are published to two decimal places. A higher-precision current-release comparison table is not silently spliced into the historical series. Rounded components need not sum exactly to the rounded index.

Kansas City exposes 14 measures under each of five comparison/adjustment blocks. Year-ago survey comparisons are diffusion responses, not computed YoY growth. Six-month expectations are observed in their survey reference month, not placed six months in the future. Seasonally adjusted diffusion values may exceed unadjusted percentage-point bounds. The original Excel date is retained; its day is not treated as an observation day or publication date. Chart coordinates use the first day of the reference month only.

The official Kansas City source page selects exactly one Historical Monthly Data XLSX link under its public documents path. Both discovery page and complete workbook are retained. No workbook formula runs and no embedded external relationship is fetched. Source schema changes, ambiguous links, duplicate months, malformed XML/ZIP data and unrepresentable numbers cannot silently change a measurement. Dataset-level shared caches and daily acquisition claims reuse one download across related series. HTTP refusal stops that dataset without retries or alternate providers. Retention failure cannot create an untraceable accepted chart.

The adapter is explicitly separate from the published warehouse catalogue and invents no stored-file endpoint. Five watchlist identifiers receive qualified official-source alternatives: USCFNAI, USCFNAISOII, USCFNAIPCHI, USKFCOMPI and USKFNORDI. Their original identifiers and source qualifications remain visible. USKFMI remains unresolved because its generic manufacturing label does not establish the exact production/composite/adjustment definition.

All observations are current revised source history. Historical release vintages, vendor equivalence, full upstream history and publication clocks remain unverified. No Calls votes, portfolio sizing authority, market candles or traded volume are introduced. Existing inputs, watchlist mappings and original assertions are preserved through an exact reversible edit ledger.
