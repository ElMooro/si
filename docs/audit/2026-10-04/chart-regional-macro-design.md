# Weekly economic activity and underlying inflation histories

Four public Dallas and Cleveland files add fourteen canonical histories while preserving the existing 644 regional Fed definitions. Dallas WEI retains the Saturday reference date, current values and seven publisher-labelled vintage columns. The index is scaled to four-quarter GDP growth; it is not realized GDP or a forecast. Labelled vintages are not independently verified release-time snapshots. Future reference weeks or vintage labels later than receipt time are withheld. Old vintage trailing blanks remain explicit nulls.

Cleveland monthly changes in decimal fractions, annualized monthly percentage changes, and published year-over-year percentage changes remain distinct series. YoY values come directly from the publisher's chart CSV, not annualizing a month or deriving an unsupported number. The October and November 2025 interpolation described by Cleveland is flagged on those monthly observations and the twelve-month windows that contain them. The chart does not describe these as wholly uninterpolated source observations. The source attribution and CC BY-SA 4.0 license are retained in definitions and displayed for Cleveland charts. The publisher's full original response is retained without losing its other columns; headline CPI and core CPI are not introduced as additional aliases.

ECONOMICS:USWEI and ECONOMICS:USCPITM receive qualified source alternatives. Vendor equivalence remains unverified; CPI Transportation is never redirected to trimmed-mean inflation. All fourteen source histories are independently reconstructed using CSV and openpyxl readers before tests; every original numeric lexeme, worksheet address, missing cell and formula-cache flag is retained. Workbook formulas and external relationships are never executed. Shared source acquisition uses four complete files across fourteen histories. Refusals stop without other-provider fallbacks. There is no Calls vote, position-sizing, market candle or volume authority.

Routing, complete received response, full upstream historical completeness, and independent release-time vintages are distinct claims. Only post-deployment native-package, public history and served-browser checks may establish live acceptance.
