Read the denominator before the ratio

The provider’s days-to-cover field has display conventions, including a floor of 1 and values of 999.99. This page also shows current position divided by reported average volume, without those display conventions. With zero average volume, the reconstructed ratio is unavailable. Neither figure predicts how long an actual exit will take.

When the previous position is zero, FINRA may report a 100% change. The reconstructed percentage is unavailable because its denominator is zero. Short-float percentage, live borrow availability and borrowing costs are not measured here.

Test your exposure assumptions

Enter your signed shares, your own USD reference price, a hypothetical price change and total costs. Negative shares represent a short position. This calculation excludes financing, dividends, taxes and currency changes. It supplies no forecast, locate or borrowing assurance.

Evidence and limits

The browser checks the recorded output and selected issue bucket. Original-source replay and independent arithmetic checks run on AWS. Complete originals remain protected; their digests, request parameters, acquisition times and row locators identify every observation. Two equal complete scans establish observed pagination stability, not an atomic provider snapshot.

Source acquisition time is not historical publication time. This record does not prove when a revised value first became available. These measurements carry zero qualified investment votes.
