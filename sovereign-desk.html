Measurements, not signals: a SovCISS, a spread or a balance says where a government's bond market stands against its own past; none of it is a forecast, a probability of default or a position size. SovCISS, CISS and CLIFS are the ECB's composite stress indicators (0 to 1). Spreads to Germany use the ECB's monthly 10-year convergence yields; the daily curve spread is all euro-area central governments minus the AAA-rated ones.
