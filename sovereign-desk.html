Measurements, not signals: a SovCISS, a spread or a balance says where a government's bond market stands against its own past; none of it is a forecast, a probability of default or a position size. SovCISS, CISS and CLIFS are the ECB's composite stress indicators (0 to 1); the ECB computes a CISS for the United States, China and the United Kingdom as well, and a SovCISS for eleven euro-area governments only. Spreads to Germany use the ECB's monthly 10-year convergence yields; the daily curve spread is all euro-area central governments minus the AAA-rated ones. CDS and ratings come from the site's world document and are not ECB data. Colour tags and sorting are kept in this browser.
