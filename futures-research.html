The first three qualifying expiry dates are a research sample, not a liquidity ranking. Near-expiry and delivery obligations require separate review. No continuous contract, roll strategy, margin requirement, independent investment vote or forecast is inferred here.

Volume counts contracts. The provider’s dollar_volume field sums quoted price times trade size without a contract multiplier; it is not USD notional.
