Accountability

Methodology — how the ultimate engine avoids fooling itself

Membership by proof, not luck. A 5-for-5 engine has a Wilson lower bound near 57% at best; seats require the 95% floor ≥55% with n≥10 — the streaks that survive statistics.

One engine, one vote. Re-logged daily signals are not new evidence; each member casts a single strongest vote per symbol, and consensus requires ≥2 distinct members, unanimous in direction, weighted by wilson×regime-fit.

Regime filtering. Members unfit in the current regime (<52% there) abstain. Slices deepen daily as the ledger's regime_at_log matures.

Self-grading. Every consensus call is written back to dynamodb:justhodl-signals as eng:alpha-council with a 21-day primary window — the council appears on the same leaderboard it draws from, founding mistakes on the books forever.
