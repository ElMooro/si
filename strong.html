Thesis · the strongest when the S&P 500 dumps

The engine tests its own thesis every run, walk-forward: for each session the universe is re-ranked from the closes through the session before by three definitions of strong — the momentum rank the desk shows (excess return vs SPY over 1d to 6M), the both-sides rank (over the trailing 63 sessions, the weaker of how the name moved on SPY's down days and on its up days — dumps less than the S&P 500 AND rallies more than it; a name needs both) and the big-days rank (the same, measured only on SPY's dump days and rally days over 126 sessions) — then every name's move on the session itself is measured against SPY. Read over the last month, the last quarter, or the year as a reference. Dump day = SPY down 1% or more, rally day = SPY up 1% or more; "held up" = flat (within 0.25%), up, or down less than SPY; industry-confirmed = a top-tenth stock whose whole industry (its members ranked here) sat in the top 30% that session. This is the record, not a forecast.

Asset classes

Every class the desk ranks — stocks, equity ETFs (broad, sector, factor), countries, bonds, high yield, cash, gold, silver, metals and miners, commodities, currencies, crypto, volatility — with its breadth against SPY, its median move today and over the week, month and quarter, how much of it held up on the last session, and its strongest and weakest names. Leveraged and inverse funds are labelled and kept out of every rank.

Long accumulation · tight price spread

The longest trailing run of sessions whose closes all sit inside 6% (VERY TIGHT, ≥ 40 sessions), 10% (TIGHT, ≥ 40), 15% (LONG BASE, ≥ 120) or 10% (FORMING, ≥ 20) of the last close — and only when that run is a contraction of the name's own yearly range (at most 60% of it), so a cash fund never coils; a stock pinned almost motionless inside a sliver of a base (ATR ≤ 0.6%, range ≤ 3.5%) trades like a pending deal and is flagged PINNED off the board. Score 0–100 = base length (35) + tightness (30) + Bollinger-width squeeze percentile (10) + volume dry-up (8) + accumulation inside the base — Chaikin money flow and up-volume / down-volume (12) — + relative strength holding through the base (5). The 13F stance and the fund's 21-observation flow sit beside the score as corroboration and never enter it.
