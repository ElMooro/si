What this composition knows

Compilation time is not the date of a market observation. Each view retains its parent publication, acquisition review deadline, field definitions and original observation dates.

Which views share evidence

These are measurement families, not independent confirmations. The population view reuses captured option contracts. The ETF desk reuses fund-flow and constituent research alongside profiles. Different recorded vintages remain separate.
