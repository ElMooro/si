Composite risk — every risk gauge in the system, two numbers, one history since 1990

Eighty-plus gauges the system already banks — credit spreads, volatility, funding, the official stress indices (OFR, St. Louis Fed, Chicago Fed, Kansas City Fed, ECB CISS and SovCISS), rates, sovereign, banking and household, uncertainty, equity and Bitcoin — are each ranked against their own history up to that day (no look-ahead), averaged into pillars and weighted into STRESS (capitulation side) and FROTH (complacency / extension side). Both are then measured against every labelled equity and Bitcoin episode. Weights and thresholds were fixed on 1990–2012; 2013-onward statistics are shown separately. Descriptive research: the packet carries no call and no sizing.
