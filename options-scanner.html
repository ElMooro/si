Contract scope: received current contracts within 14–90 calendar days and ±10% of the matching reported spot request gate. At most ten closest valid unique calls and ten puts are selected. Every returned contract is retained; pagination and request failures are explicit.

Volume: option bars report traded contracts. Same-date ratios require one valid volume observation for every selected call and put and complete returned contract pagination. Missing sessions never become zero, and zero puts never become a denominator of one. Historical membership, session completeness and adjustments remain unverified.

Separate FINRA scope: daily reported off-exchange shares. ShortVolume includes exempt trades; daily trading volume is not outstanding short interest, covering or borrowing.

Unknown: buying versus selling, opening versus closing, spread legs, investor identity, open interest and implied volatility. No bullish tier, independent investment vote or position sizing follows from these records.

Original evidence: every received response has an exact byte length, SHA-256 and whole-source viewer. Denied and unattempted requests are visible. Collection time is not an original release vintage.
