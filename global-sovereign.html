Definitions that must be qualified before comparison
  • 10Y yield: instrument, currency, maturity/tenor, price convention and quote time. Cross-currency yield differences include more than credit risk.
  • CDS: currency, tenor, restructuring clause, quote type and time. Missing CDS cannot become zero risk. The provider's default-probability field needs its horizon and recovery assumptions.
  • Spread field: verify the actual reference instrument, tenor and unit. The inherited “vs Bund” interpretation has not been established from original responses.
  • Policy rate and rating: verify the instrument, issuing agency, effective date and publication date. One country-level date cannot qualify every field.
