Daily short-sale reporting

Short volume includes short-exempt trades. The denominator is FINRA-disseminated regular-session TRF/ADF volume in exchange-listed securities. It is not consolidated all-market volume, short interest or buying pressure.

Weekly ATS and non-ATS activity

Combined activity requires matching reported issue, week and tier. Missing legs remain unavailable. ATS percentage here is a share of reported off-exchange activity. Average shares per reported trade does not identify block trades, retail flow or investor accumulation.

Monthly reporting-firm concentration

The de-minimis row combines undisclosed reporting firms. HHI therefore appears as an interval: the known firms' contribution is the lower bound; treating the undisclosed bucket as one firm gives the upper bound. This measures reporting activity, not beneficial ownership.

Test your exposure assumptions

Enter signed shares, your own USD reference price, a hypothetical price change and total costs. Negative shares represent a short position. The result excludes dividends, financing, taxes and currency changes. Trade-reporting data does not supply a price forecast or a fill estimate.

Evidence and calculation limits

The browser verifies the recorded publication and selected data bucket. Original-source reconstruction is performed on the runner. Provider originals stay protected; their digests, acquisition clocks and source row locations remain attached to the displayed records.
