📚 Methodology  ·  v3 Multi-Pillar Composite Cycle Indicator

Each economy's cycle read is a composite of five pillars built from JustHodl's own warehouses, refreshed daily by the justhodl-cycle-features feature store: survey (OECD Composite Leading Indicator where published, OECD business + consumer confidence, order books and production expectations, Eurostat ESI / consumer / industry confidence for EU members), financial (long-minus-short interest rate curve, BIS private-credit impulse, real house prices, real broad effective exchange rate, broad money growth, policy-rate change), activity (industrial production and retail volume y/y, unemployment 12-month change), trade (merchandise exports and imports y/y, export order books) and equity (the v2 momentum composite). Every feature is standardised against its own prior history (up to 120 months, at least 36, clipped ±3, sign-adjusted for its cyclical direction), carried forward for at most its publication lag (monthly 4 months, quarterly 7) and otherwise excluded rather than filled in. Pillars are averaged, then combined with weights survey 0.35 · financial 0.25 · activity 0.20 · trade 0.10 · equity 0.10, renormalised over the pillars each country actually has; CLI = 100 + 20 × tanh(z / 2). A country needs at least two non-equity pillars to be classified from the composite (tagged otherwise); its equity-only read is always kept alongside. The 6-month downturn probability is a logistic fit of the GDP-weighted composite and its 3-month change against "GDP-weighted industrial production y/y six months ahead < 0" over the platform's own monthly history since 2004 — an in-sample calibration shown with its AUC, not a validated forecast.

Equity pillar (v2.1, kept in full): the OECD CLI series via FRED stopped in January 2024, so the equity-momentum synthetic CLI remains the daily, real-time pillar and the fallback wherever the feature store is thin.

Inputs: Yahoo Finance daily price history for each country's primary equity index (e.g. ^GSPC for USA, ^N225 for Japan, ^GDAXI for Germany, ^FTSE for UK, ^BSESN for India, 000001.SS for China, EPOL for Poland, ECH for Chile). Every price candidate must clear the same acceptance gate (≥ 252 daily bars and a last bar ≤ 12 days old). When a market's index symbol is dead on the source, the engine walks a fixed chain: alternate index symbols → an equal-weight blue-chip basket of that exchange (tagged basket) → the US-listed country ETF from JustHodl's own polygon-full warehouse (tagged ETF proxy) → the freshest stale candidate, explicitly tagged degraded → no data. Nothing is ever filled in. For USA + China, a fresh (≤ 3 months) OECD Consumer/Business Confidence reading (USACSCICP02STSAM, CHNBSCICP02STSAM) is blended 75/25 as an anomaly from its 100 normal; a stale reading is excluded and reported as such.

Physical confirmation: each label is cross-checked against IMF PortWatch port throughput (median YoY across ≥ 2 ports): ⚓ confirmed when trade volume agrees with the equity-derived phase, ⚓ divergent when it contradicts it. If the port feed ships empty, the previous run's read is carried forward and marked as such rather than erased.

Composite formula: composite_pct = 0.35×ret_12m + 0.25×dist_200ma + 0.25×ret_3m + 0.15×ret_1m; CLI = 100 + 20 × tanh(0.025 × composite_pct) — slope 0.5 at the 100 boundary (identical to the v2.0 linear map) and soft-bounded to (80, 120), so strong markets keep their ordering instead of piling up on a hard 120 cap. Each country is then classified into EXPANSION, AT_RISK, RECESSION, or RECOVERY based on the CLI level (vs. trend of 100) and the 3-month return direction. The breadth across countries — GDP-weighted — drives the global phase, which propagates into the Khalid Index, risk dashboard, allocator, morning brief, and AI chat.
