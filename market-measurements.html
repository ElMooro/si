Measurement boundaries

Equity returns compare observed closes on calendar-based dates, with the actual baseline shown. They exclude dividends and transaction costs. The bounded history does not establish an all-time high or historical point-in-time availability. CoinGecko percentage changes and historical maxima are provider-reported; their baselines have not been independently reconstructed.

Freshness uses stated observation and acquisition age ceilings. A verified exchange release calendar and complete security-master history are still required. Missing and stale observations remain visible as unavailable; they do not become zero returns.
