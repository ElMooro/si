What these measurements mean


Money-market assets are a stock. A weekly change does not identify transfers into equities or establish cash waiting to buy risk assets. Long-term figures combine estimates of mutual-fund cash flows with ETF net issuance; they are not a pure ETF-flow measure.


Release date, observation date and acquisition time are separate. The complete retrieved release is retained for replay, but it can include revisions to earlier weeks. Legacy histories without verified release vintages are preserved separately. Year-on-year growth, 13-week statistics and predictive z-scores remain unavailable.


Portfolio consequence: unavailable. WAIT means abstain. This engine has no qualified Calls vote, position size or execution permission. Research measurements require separate out-of-sample validation and portfolio risk information before supporting a trade.


Refresh reads the existing publication. Failed source requests or invalid measurements preserve the last verified packet and its original timestamp. An unavailable feed is never displayed as zero.
