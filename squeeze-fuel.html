Evidence and interpretation

The browser verifies the recorded output and selected CUSIP bucket. Original-source replay and independent row/arithmetic checks run on AWS. Complete original responses remain protected, with their hashes, acquisition times and exact line numbers retained in the research record.

Provider archive links identify the source; they are not a guarantee that the provider still serves the captured bytes. Acquisition time does not establish when a historical value first became public. The selected files contain reported dates, not a verified complete trading calendar.

Reported prices refer to the prior day; a dot means unavailable or sub-penny. Currency and the exact price observation date are not explicit in the source. The sum of file quantities is an integrity checksum, never a flow or a cumulative outstanding balance.
