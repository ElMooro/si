CAPITULATION · PANIC VOLUME AT THE LOWS

One session on any instrument the bottom desk scores: a down close at or under the asset class's floor, volume at least 2.5× the prior 50 sessions and the heaviest of 20, a range at least 1.5 ATR, the close in the bottom 30% of the bar, at the prior 20-session low or a crash. Every print is scored, read in context (a sell-off already underway, or an event shock), followed through (did the low hold, did the volume dry up) and linked to the Wyckoff sequence when it is the selling-climax bar. The breadth of prints across the whole universe, against five years of sessions, is the market-wide read. The bottom engine computes all of it each morning at 03:45 UTC from the Polygon EOD bank and the crypto bank. A print is where the crowd gave up — it is not a buy.
