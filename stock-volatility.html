STOCK VOLATILITY & PRICE SPREAD

Every listed common stock and plain ETF, measured each morning from the Polygon EOD bank. Volatility is realized: the standard deviation of daily returns over the last 21, 63 and 252 sessions, stated in percent a year. Price spread is how far the highest high of a window stood above its lowest low, for the last day, week (5 sessions), month (21) and quarter (63). Every column sorts both ways, so any of them ranks the market.
