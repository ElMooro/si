      📐 Methodology

      how the score is computed

      Measurements above come from the reported Treasury auction and buyback sources. Compare observation dates separately from engine publication time; a polling schedule is not a guaranteed publication latency.

      The six detector patterns are legacy descriptive heuristics. Selected crisis examples do not establish calibration, prospective accuracy, causal market impact or portfolio sizing. AAH denotes marginal-bid proration; it is not dealer absorption or a when-issued tail.

      The composite summarizes those heuristics. Historical conditional estimates require retained source data and a reproducible evaluation before display. No claim of a live historical warning or absence of backtest bias is made here.

      Security kind, quote basis and reopening status must agree for comparisons. Buyback accepted par and settlement cash are separate quantities. Inspect the current measurement packet.
