SPX Beaters — The Weekly League

Every Saturday the engine scans every listed stock — large, mid, small and micro cap — every ETF and the asset-class board, and lists the candidates with evidence-backed higher odds of beating the S&P 500, with the why spelled out per name. Momentum is computed in-house across the full market (the most replicated systematic SPX-beating factor); the rest of the evidence comes from the fleet's screeners, 13F and congress flows, the industry boom league, and the sp500 pillar composite. Scores are weighted odds from real evidence — not guarantees. Macro gates sizing, never selection.
