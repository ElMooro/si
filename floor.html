Doctrine: a dump is only sensible if the balance sheet allows it. Every filer is screened daily against its own liquid floor (cash + investments + live-marked crypto − debt) and its committed order book. Each drawdown is split into asset-driven (a treasury should fall when its coin falls) and residual — the part nothing on the balance sheet explains. A large residual against a well-covered floor is senseless. But a discount is only worth owning if the floor survives: burn, dilution and debt are checked before any discount is credited, and they can veto the call outright. Custody crypto never counts. Every leg carries its XBRL tag and filing date.
